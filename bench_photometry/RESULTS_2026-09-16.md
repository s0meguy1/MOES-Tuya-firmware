# Bench photometry results, 2026-09-16 — the fade-to-off dropout

Answers to `AI_DROPOUT_BRIEF.md` §5 tests A, C and D on `moes_bench`
(the bench fixture), **build 40**, 230 mireds. Test B (stock on the same jig)
has not been run. Raw data: `results/*.csv`; reducers: `analyze_sweep.py`,
`analyze_fade.py`; expected duty per level: `expected.py`.

**Instrument.** BH1750 (photopic, MTreg 31, ~54 ms integration) and TSL2591
(1x, 100 ms) on the bench Pi, fixed geometry, white paper under the sensors,
fixture directly above. Ambient with the light off: BH 7 counts, TSL 6 counts.
Level 254: BH 48 689, TSL ch0 18 255 — neither sensor within 25% of saturation.
Every number below is a ratio to level 254 of the same run; the standard
deviation of 10 reads is quoted with each mean. Up and down sweeps agree within
0.3% at every level; fixture drift at level 254 is under 0.5% per 15 min.

**Two things that silently corrupt this measurement, both hit today:**

* The z2m external converter (`moes_ts0505b_light_show.js`) carries
  `BRIGHTNESS_FLOOR = 13`: any `brightness` below 13 is sent as 13. Levels 1-12
  are only reachable with a raw ZCL command
  (`{"command":{"cluster":"genLevelCtrl","command":"moveToLevel"...}}`).
* `moveToLevelWithOnOff` to level 1 switches the light **off** (ZCL minimum-level
  rule, `zcl_levelCb.c` `tuyaLight_levelWithOnOffChk`). Level 1 lit needs plain
  `moveToLevel`. The first "level 1 is dark" reading was this, not the driver.

* **Light is linear in duty from level 1 to about level 48**, slope 2.85-2.97

* **The driver saturates above roughly level 64.** Levels 64-254 span only
  77-100% of light. Three quarters of the level range carries a quarter of the
  light. This is a separate finding from the cliff and affects every scene above
  level ~90; it is recorded here, not acted on.
* The ratio TSL/BH drifts between levels 24 and 144 and the TSL IR fraction
  (ch1/ch0) rises from 0.20 to 0.35 around level 64 and falls back by 128: the
  cool-white channel saturates before the warm one, so the colour temperature
  drifts warmer through the middle of the range and back. Photopic (BH) numbers
  are the ones used above.
* Open question 4 (is the map linear in output?) is answered: linear in duty
  only up to ~level 48, then compressive.

## C. Bottom repeatability (`results/C_b40_bottom.csv`, `A1_b40_plainlevel.csv`)

Level 1, plain move, 50 reads over 8.5 s: BH 10 613 ± 6.8 (**0.064%**).
Levels 2-16 in the sweep: sd 0.013-0.05%. **The driver is steady down to level
1 (1.19% absolute duty).** Open question 2 is closed: levels 1-5 are stable, the
cubic's dwell there is not a problem. Where the driver stops regulating (open

One-off: during test C the light switched itself off ~10 s after a plain

## D. Fade shape (`results/D_8s_off.csv`, `D_20s_off.csv`, 85 samples/s)

Measured light versus what `tuyaLight_levelPaced()` intends, using the measured
curve from A to translate intended level into intended light:

| 8 s fade, t | intended level | intended light | measured light | perceived (cbrt) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.0 s | 107 | 89.0% | 89.0% | 96% |
| 4.0 s | 31.8 | 42.3% | 42.5% | 75% |
| 6.0 s | 3.97 | 10.0% | 10.2% | 47% |
| 6.6-7.7 s | 1.0 -> 0.01 | 4% -> 0 | **5.6-6.6% plateau** | **38-40%** |
| 7.8-7.9 s | 0 | 0 | 2.4% -> 0 (54 ms integration smear) | -> 0 |

* **The cubic pacing works exactly as designed on hardware**: intended and
  measured light agree to 0.3 points from 254 down to level 4.
* **The cliff is the floor.** Once the intended level drops below 1 the output
  is clamped at the brightmin floor: 5.7% of full light = **38% of the
  perceived range**, held for 1.1 s (8 s fade) or 1.7 s (20 s fade), then cut to
  black in one step. The brief estimated 26%; the driver's saturation makes the
  floor 5.7% light rather than 1.3%, so the step is 38%.
* The 20 s fade is identical in shape (plateau 17.5-19.2 s at 5.65%, cut).
* The plateau is not flat: it slopes from 6.6% to 5.6% light (8 s fade) because
  build 40 already paces its internal 8.8 level to zero on a level-0 command;
  what holds the output up is `moes_dimCurve256()`, whose brightmin term floors
  every non-zero input at 1.0% duty (1.39% at exactly level 1). Just before
  black there is a 0.1-0.3 s step at ~3.3%: the warm channel's share of the
  split reaches zero a few frames before the cool channel's, so one channel cuts
  first. Both effects vanish if the curve ramps its floor out below one level,
  which is the build-41 change in `moes_dim.c`.

## What this says about the fix

Build 39 paced the output below the floor to zero; build 40 removed that on a
field trace that reported instability under 1% duty, from an instrument that
could not resolve it. The photometry shows the driver linear and steady down to
the lowest duty build 40 emits, and shows the plateau-and-cut to be the whole
artefact. **Build 41 (bench only, `light/moes_dim.c` + `light/zcl_levelCb.c`)
restores the sub-floor extinction tail; the steady map for levels 1..254 is
byte-for-byte unchanged.** Whether the driver holds below 1% duty is what the
build-41 fade recordings measure. Possible outcomes: light follows the cubic to
black (cliff gone); light cuts at some lower duty (cliff smaller, e.g. ~1.7%
light = 26% perceived); or slow instability shows as scatter in the tail. Fast
(kHz) flicker is outside what a 54 ms integration can see.

## Builds 41-43 on the bench (same evening)

* **Build 41** (per-channel ramp-out below one level): 8 s fade tracks the intended curve to
  black, last lit sample 0.6% of full (18% perceived), monotone at 54 ms resolution, no
  plateau. **But steady levels 1-4 dimmed** (level 1 by 37%, level 2 by 18%) because the
  warm channel's split share is below one level there. Rejected for the fleet.
* **Build 42/43** (`moes_dimTail256()`: the level-1 rendering scaled by the sub-level fraction,
  after the curve): 8 s fade on b43 goes monotonically to black, last lit sample 0.7% of full
  (19% perceived), no plateau (`results/D43_8s_off.csv`); steady levels untouched by
  construction (the tail is the identity at and above one level, host-tested).
* **Stored shows (b43):** uploads of 1, 5, 7, 8, 14, 20, 21, 22 and 23 entries all counted
  exactly; save to slots 0/1/3, recall of each, and recall+run in one frame all behaved; after
  a mains cycle the fixture reported slot 0's list with no upload and played it
  (`show_test.py` phases a/b, `show_test_c.py`, `show_test_d.py`).

Not measured: test B (stock). The driver saturation and the colour drift make
that comparison less informative than the brief assumed - stock shares the
driver, so it has the same 5.7% floor and the same 38% step.
